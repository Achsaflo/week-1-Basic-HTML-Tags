Key characteristics of AI systems

Machine Learning (ML):

Machine learning (ML) is a subset of artificial intelligence that focuses on creating algorithms capable of learning patterns from data and making predictions or decisions without explicit programming for each task.
By training on historical datasets, these models generalize to unseen data, enabling automation and intelligent decision-making across industries.
At its core, ML works by feeding data into algorithms,
adjusting internal parameters to minimize prediction errors, and producing a trained model that can classify
predict, or detect patterns in new inputs. This process can be applied to structured data (e.g., financial records)
